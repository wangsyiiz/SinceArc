Why another client

Many agents, one timeline

Several CLI agents, group members and their sessions sit on a single timeline. Mention a member with @ and it runs on its own branch in parallel, then merges back into the trunk — no more copying context between terminal windows, and one place to review what each of them did.

Process apart from prose

Replies stay in the chat; thinking, tool calls and permission prompts go into a trace panel. The two cross-link by anchor, so "why did it do that" always has an answer.

Local first

Sessions, traces and settings live in %APPDATA%\com.sincearc.app. Nothing is uploaded, and the client works fully without an account.
